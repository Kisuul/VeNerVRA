# Contributing and authority boundaries

1. Identify the authorized question, target investigation and current revision. Read [VEN.md](VEN.md).
2. Do not change authoritative contract, research protocols, CI, permissions, or sensitive research without the corresponding explicit scope and independent review.
3. Research content lives under `research/<investigation-id>/`. Use templates and index records; preserve provenance and contrary evidence.
4. Do not publish private information, unreviewed personal conversation excerpts, credentials, or restricted sources to a public repository. Use private storage when appropriate.
5. Propose reusable skills in KestrelGaming through its accepted branch/review workflow; proposals in `integration/` are not active skills.
6. For substantive changes run the validator, unit tests and complete diff review. Report exact checks, omissions and the revision inspected.
7. Concurrent contributors must reconcile current remote changes. Do not force-push another contributor's records. An unmerged branch is not the shared default branch.
8. Research design feedback alone is not permission for remote writes. No automatic publication or background work is implied.

For small corrections, prefer small diffs. For contested facts, preserve conflicting records and reopen the question rather than selecting the newer statement by date.
