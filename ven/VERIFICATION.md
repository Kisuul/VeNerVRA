# Research verification and integrity

## Evidence ladder

1. **Provenance** — Identify the actual artifact, author/source, date, data origin and inspected revision.
2. **Internal coherence** — Check definitions, units, formal logic, transformations and math, where relevant.
3. **Independent corroboration** — Examine source dependence and alternative explanations; count truly independent evidence carefully.
4. **Discriminating checks** — Establish negative cases, counterexamples, measurement sensitivity and falsifiable predictions.
5. **Reproducibility** — Preserve conditions, commands, data versions, test inputs and environmental differences; do not fabricate an execution.
6. **Adversarial review** — Use separate reviewer or blinded test where decision stakes warrant it.
7. **Communication** — Ensure final citations support the exact stated claims and clearly flag unknowns.

Structural validators only verify repository shape; they cannot demonstrate factual correctness, source authority, behavior, reasoning quality, or permission compliance.

## Prohibited research shortcuts

Never alter a baseline, silently weaken a discriminating test, erase negative results, describe retrieved secondary commentary as firsthand source inspection, or elevate a plausible inference to a supported observation without evidence. A false citation is worse than an explicit unsourced statement.

## Research task completion

Complete when the authorized research *deliverable or bounded investigation* is adequately supported within its declared scope. Do not require ultimate certainty. If inconclusive, report PARTIAL with the next discriminating check. For severe evidence gaps, report BLOCKED only when no useful authorized progress remains.
