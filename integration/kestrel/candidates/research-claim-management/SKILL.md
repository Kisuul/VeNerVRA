# Manage claims and hypotheses without belief inflation

**Target location after authorized import:** `skills/research-claim-management/SKILL.md` in KestrelGaming. **Candidate, NOT ACTIVE.** Respect the owning investigation's actual schema and authority.

## Inputs and triggers

An investigation question, existing claims and sources, user-stated hypotheses, available tests, and authorized storage scope. Activate when conclusions may evolve or alternatives must be compared. Do not manufacture a claim database for one-off trivial answers.

## Procedure

1. **Identify proposition:** One precise claim with definitions, conditions and scope. Mark user-asserted or proposed content explicitly.
2. **Connect evidence:** Supporting and contradicting sources, derivation for inferences, known dependencies, and claims still UNSOURCED.
3. **Separate statuses:** Memory lifecycle is not evidentiary truth. Use `proposed`, `investigating`, `supported`, `disputed`, `refuted` or `superseded` according to actual evidence.
4. **Specify discrimination:** Identify an observable result that would distinguish the target from viable alternatives; record limits of the proposed test.
5. **Update honestly:** When new evidence contradicts prior belief, preserve the original record or version; do not silently redefine a failed hypothesis. An alternate claim requires a distinct identity.
6. **Stop:** Report adequate scoped result, inconclusive findings, or blocker. Do not infer objective truth from an internally consistent graph.
7. **Persist only when authorized:** Use the target repository's accepted record and indexing procedures; report local vs synchronized state.

## Output

**Claim + conditions / epistemic state / supporting and contrary evidence / alternative / discriminating check / recorded change**. For a routine response, a few sentences are sufficient.

## Worked cases (not measured)

1. **Null result:** A planned experiment finds no expected effect. Preserve the negative observation, narrow the claim only by explicit versioned revision, and avoid declaring that every related hypothesis is refuted.
2. **Authority collision:** Two contradictory articles with different dates. Preserve both and identify the source or experiment resolving the discrepancy; do not pick the newest by default.
3. **Premature support promotion:** A source mentions a speculative mechanism. Mark mechanism speculative rather than supported, even if a citation exists.
